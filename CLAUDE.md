# The Panhandler - A Raspberry Pi Travel Router & Passive Web Scanner a Nixtinctive design 

## IDENTITY
From the mind of the user: LowCache@volnix & in collaboration with, the Louise to lowcache's Thelma, Claude. Speeding off the cliff to their utter demise, hand in hand...
This is the overview and instructions for The Panhandler - a raspberry pi travel router built to walk into hostile public networks and come back clean. It expects the worst the local coffee-shop radio can throw at it: captive portals that lie, DNS that misdirects, and the odd someone poking at it from the next table over - and it's hardened to shrug all of that off. 
It also keeps its hand out as a passive observer - watching what the network around it leaks into the open, the things disclosed accidentally, on purpose, or by design that are more than meet's the eye. 
Personal protection, intellectual property, interdimensional beings, time-travelevers, big foot - everybody's offering something, and the Panhandler is just standing there with its hand out 

## DIRECTION & SCOPE
For the Panhandler, start with per previous instructions: `.memory/` 
then in the `docs/` folder read the files (do not skim):
- `BLUEPRINT.md` 
- `HARDENING.md` 
- `RISKS.md`  
- `THREAT-MODEL.md`

this is a nix based project, with nix being installed on the Raspberry Pi as well as the volnix system its being developed on 
so the `nixtastic` skill and the `mcp-nixos` tools under `mcp-gateway` will be necessary

to maintain 5h & 7d session health, and to keep from getting rate limited in the middle of a task token heavy tasks that would 
drain the 5h sessio window should be delegated using `tether` and given to Gemini pro account with all the necessary factors to 
ensure a quality output, while maintaining standards and always checking the output for errors and hallucinations. 

## HARDWARE 
this project has the use of:

- Raspberry Pi 3b 
- 2x 32GB sdcard (one card has the pi image but the second is empty and available) 
- sdcard reader
- volnix laptop - this system
- Volatile Device - galaxy s26 ultra verizon variant
- ethernet link/chord from pi to laptop
- 15" monitor attached with HDMI as 2nd screen to volnix or can be used to troubleshoot screen output on pi
